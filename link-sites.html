Link your resume, portfolio and profile

Linking puts the other sites on each site's menu — a Portfolio, Profile or Resume link (or a floating button when the design has no menu). Fill in at least two sites; every pair is linked both ways.

Normally this happens automatically when you publish both sites from the same browser. Use this page if you published on another device, cleared your browser data, or the link did not appear.
